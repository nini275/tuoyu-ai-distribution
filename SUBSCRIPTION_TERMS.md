# Subscription Terms

Effective date: October 21, 2026

These terms apply to plan previews, the Pro trial, and future Microsoft Store subscriptions for TuoYu (the "Service"). The current build is a local browser prototype and is not connected to real payments, purchase receipts, or renewal management.

## 1. Service Description

The Service helps publishers organize public facts about software, models, and open-source projects, match projects with suitable distribution channels, generate editable channel content, and support review, scheduling, and manual publishing records.

The Service improves a project's clarity and the likelihood that it will be found and cited, but it does not control retrieval, ranking, or responses in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, or other third-party products. It does not guarantee a specific ranking, inclusion, traffic, or commercial result.

## 2. Current Prototype Status

Before production Microsoft Store billing is integrated:

- Plan switching in the app is only for local interface and quota testing.
- The app does not charge a fee, create a real subscription, or enable automatic renewal.
- The "seven-day free trial" and plan previews are stored in the current browser and are not Microsoft Store purchase records.
- Clearing browser site data may reset the local plan, quota, and trial state.

A production build must use Microsoft Store to display products, prices, and payment confirmation, and a trusted server must validate purchase receipts.

## 3. Prices and Billing Periods

The currently planned prices are:

- Creator: ¥49 per month or ¥399 per year.
- Pro: ¥149 per month or ¥1,299 per year.
- Free: ¥0.

After release, the currency, amount, taxes, billing period, and discounts shown on the Microsoft Store purchase page at confirmation are authoritative. Annual plans renew or expire according to the selected Store product's term. Monthly quotas reset on the first day of each natural month in China Standard Time, and unused quota does not roll over.

See [PRICING.md](./PRICING.md) for detailed quota and usage rules.

## 4. Trial

When a production Store trial is available:

- A single Store account or verified workspace may claim the seven-day Pro trial only once.
- The trial does not automatically convert to a paid subscription unless the user explicitly confirms a purchase on the Store page.
- When the trial expires, the Service returns to Free-plan quotas; existing data is not automatically deleted.
- The project team may adjust or end the trial under applicable law and Store policy, but will not retroactively charge for trial periods expressly identified as free.

## 5. Automatic Renewal and Cancellation

After production subscriptions launch, Microsoft Store manages automatic renewal, payment methods, cancellation controls, and renewal dates. Users should cancel or change subscriptions in the subscription-management section of their Microsoft account.

After cancellation, the Service generally continues through the end of the current paid period. Whether a completed billing period is refundable is governed by Microsoft Store policy, the product description, and mandatory consumer-protection law.

## 6. Quotas and Fair Use

Users may not abuse the Service by bypassing quotas, forging purchase receipts, sharing accounts, conducting automated attacks, making malicious refund requests, or using other improper methods. If anomalous activity is detected, related features may be paused during a reasonable investigation, while users must retain a channel to object and recover data as required by law.

Downgrading a plan does not delete existing data, but it pauses new actions that exceed the new plan's limits. A generation batch that fails without producing content does not consume channel-content quota.

The current local prototype does not upload project records to TuoYu servers. See [PRIVACY.md](./PRIVACY.md) for data processing related to browser storage.
